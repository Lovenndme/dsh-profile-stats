A personal usage profile page in the DSH sidebar, folded from this machine's own session logs through ctx.sessionQuery. Five headline numbers (tokens with a small trend and the change against the previous period, spend, longest task, the active-day streak with your best, today's cache hit rate), a 12-month activity grid in daily / weekly / running-total views, a model-family chart by day, week or month as stacked columns or a share pie (version and tier stay apart, and vendor-specific ids collapse correctly), a token-stats ring chart of where the tokens went (system prompt, tool definitions, user messages, injected context, skills, tool calls, reasoning, answers), tokens and spend per project with the subagent share, and a per-provider split with each route's own hit rate and spend. A range switch in the header (today, this week, this month, all time or custom dates) drives the token and spend numbers, the token stats, the projects and the providers, and the selected range exports as CSV or JSON. Spend is priced at bundled list prices (peak and off-peak hours for DeepSeek direct); each provider can be set to list price, a discount, a monthly plan or not counted, stored only on this machine. The only outbound request is a six-hourly read of this package's own version from npm for the in-panel update button - no telemetry, no credentials, nothing uploaded.
